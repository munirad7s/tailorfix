## Theory the course builds on (session 1)

- Innovation as "a novel combination of purpose and means and the result of a problem-solving process" (Reichwald & Piller 2009); dimensions of innovation (Hauschildt 2004).
- Closed vs. open innovation (Chesbrough 2003; Chesbrough & Bogers 2014); open innovation happens via challenges, crowdsourcing, ecosystems, co-creation.
- Platforms are "facilitators of exchange between different types of actors that could not otherwise transact with each other; they mediate transactions across different actor groups subject to network effects" (Rochet & Tirole 2003, 2006; Evans, Hagiu & Schmalensee 2006; Armstrong 2006).
- Design Thinking (Tim Brown, IDEO) as the structure: empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test, with customer involvement in every step.
