# Interview guides

Rules: ask about past behaviour, not hypothetical love for the idea (Mom Test). No leading questions. Ask permission before recording; otherwise take notes. No names in this repository; findings are aggregated in docs/06.

## Screener (customers)

1. Ordered fashion online at least twice in the last 12 months?
2. Returned at least one item for fit reasons in the last 12 months?
3. Ever had a garment altered (any source)?

Target: yes to 1 and 2; 3 is a bonus. Aim for a mix of ages and both suits/dresses and everyday wear.

## Customer interview (15 minutes)

1. Tell me about the last fashion item you sent back. What was wrong with it, exactly? (probe: how much too long / loose; what did you do next: re-order, other shop, gave up)
2. How did the return go for you: effort, time, packaging, drop-off?
3. Have you ever kept something that did not fit perfectly? What did you do with it?
4. When did you last use a tailor? How did you find them, what did it cost, how long did it take?
5. Show the offer screen (prototype): What do you think this is? What would you do? Why?
6. What would make you say no? (distance, time, trust, hassle, cost beyond the voucher)
7. If the tailor could not fix it, what would you expect from Zalando?
8. Demographics in ranges only: age range, how often you buy online, Zalando Plus yes/no.

## Tailor interview (20 minutes, in the shop)

1. What share of your work is alterations versus made-to-measure? Which jobs are most common?
2. Do customers already bring online purchases? How often, which items, from which shops?
3. Price list for: trouser hem, waist in/out, sleeve shortening (shirt, jacket), dress hem, jacket sides. Typical turnaround.
4. Capacity: how many extra jobs per week could you take? Busy and quiet seasons?
5. Would you accept prepaid vouchers from a platform? At which rate relative to your list price? What settlement terms (weekly, monthly) would be acceptable?
6. What would you need from Zalando: certification visit, standard price list, app, support line?
7. What worries you? (dependence, disputes, quality expectations, garments arriving unworn with tags)
8. Have you worked with any platform or retailer before (Breuninger, Wöhrl, P&C, Repair Rebels, Sojo-like services)?

## Expert interview (30 minutes, remote OK)

For returns researchers, supply-chain and marketing chairs, retail associations, former repair-service operators.

1. What does one fashion return cost a platform like Zalando today, by component? Which number would you use for 2026?
2. Which share of fit returns is "small alteration" territory, in your data or experience?
3. Zalando piloted Care & Repair in 2021 and paused it. What do you think happened, and what would have to be different?
4. Where do you see the biggest risk in a subsidised alteration voucher: cannibalisation, fraud, tailor quality, brand buy-in, legal (warranty after alteration)?
5. How would you launch it: cities, categories, partner acquisition, pricing between brand and platform?
6. Which KPI would convince Zalando's management after a 3-month pilot?
